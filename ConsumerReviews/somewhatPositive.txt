After having a delightful day in Charlottesville, we were so excited to see that there was a Melting Pot there. We called and were able to get reservations. The ambience of the resturant is pleasing and modern. We were promptly seated and then greeted by our server, Larry. He was knowledgeable about the menu and explained in detail the different choices from cheeses to cooking broths. Fondues are one of our favorite family/social meals as it takes time to cook and gives the party time to talk and interact. No phones - just good ole conversation! The cheese fondue was my favorite - but the main entree of proteins was delicious. We were so full we didn't get a chance to try the chocolate -but we will be back.
