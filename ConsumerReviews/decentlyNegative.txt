I went to this escape room to celebrate my three year anniversary. It was the worst escape room I've ever visited. With most escape rooms, staff members monitor you with a video camera, and you just call them when you want hints. In this escape room, staff members kept entering our room and giving us hints, when we really wanted to solve the puzzles on our own. In the first 30 minutes we were in the room, 2 staff members entered (without us asking them to), gave clues (unprompted), and exited, each staying around 5 minutes. However, the latter part of our time at the room was the worst. One staff member stayed in the room with us for 15 minutes and another was there for around 20 minutes. They'd literally hover over us as we were trying to piece together clues. If you took longer than a minute to solve a puzzle, they'd give you the answer (unprompted). And, you wouldn't even have time to look around the room and contemplate the next clue-- they'd walk over to the clue and literally hold it in front of you as if you were a child. I wasn't able to talk or make jokes to my girlfriend because I felt awkward that I was being watched by a stranger standing 2 feet away from me. This completely took the fun out of the experience and was a huge waste of $56.
